Insta Unfollow — Followers & Unfollowers Tracker

Who doesn't follow you back? Who's secretly a bot? Insta Unfollow analyses your Instagram account and answers both questions in seconds — then unfollows the accounts you choose, safely and politely.

Key features:
• Non-followers finder — see exactly who isn't following you back, in one clean list
• Bot & fake-account scanner — suspicious accounts flagged with a risk score, plus deep-scan checks
• Safe unfollowing — respectful pacing so Instagram doesn't throttle you; pause or stop anytime
• Whitelist — protect friends, family, and accounts you never want to touch
• Growth tracking — see who unfollowed you since your last analysis
• Free plan — 40 analyses on day one, +20 every day, 50 unfollows per day
• Premium (optional) — unlimited analysis and unlimited unfollowing

How it works:
1. Sign in with Google
2. Open Instagram in your browser
3. Click Analyse — your followers and following lists are read
4. Review, whitelist who you love, unfollow the rest

Your data stays yours: results are stored on your device. We never touch your private messages, stories, or posts.
