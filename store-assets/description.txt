Insta Unfollow — Followers & Unfollowers Tracker

Who doesn't follow you back? Find out in seconds. Insta Unfollow is an unfollowers tracker for Instagram: it reads your followers and following lists, shows exactly who isn't following you back, and lets you unfollow them safely — with a bot / fake follower scan built in.

Key features:
• Non-followers finder — see exactly who isn't following you back, in one clean list
• Search, sort and filter — find any account by name, sort A–Z or not-following-first, filter by following-back / whitelisted
• Export CSV & copy usernames — take your non-followers list with you, no server involved
• Bot & fake follower scan — suspicious accounts flagged with a risk score and the reasons behind it, plus deep-scan profile checks
• Safe unfollowing — respectful pacing so Instagram doesn't throttle you; pause or stop anytime, with a countdown to the next unfollow
• Whitelist — protect friends, family, and accounts you never want to touch
• Growth tracking — see how your not-following count changed since your last analysis
• Free plan — 40 analyses on day 1, +20 every day, 50 unfollows per day
• Premium (optional) — unlimited analysis and unlimited unfollowing

How it works:
1. Sign in with Google
2. Open Instagram in your browser
3. Click Analyse — your followers and following lists are read
4. Review, whitelist who you love, unfollow the rest

Your data stays yours: results are stored on your device. We never touch your private messages, stories, or posts. No account is ever unfollowed without you choosing it.
