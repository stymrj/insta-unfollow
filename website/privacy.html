Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Insta Unfollow - Followers & Unfollowers Tracker Chrome extension ("the extension", "we") handles information. We respect your privacy and designed the extension to keep your data on your device.

1. Data we access

To function, the extension accesses the following, only after you explicitly sign in:

  • Google account — your name, email address and profile picture (basic profile scope), used solely to identify you and manage your plan.
  • Instagram data — your follower and following lists, and public profile details, read through your own logged-in Instagram session in your browser.

2. Data we store

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's local storage. This includes your sign-in information, Instagram analysis results, and settings. It never leaves your device.

3. Data we do NOT collect or share

  • We do not operate servers that collect or store your Instagram or Google data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We do not use tracking or analytics that identify you.

4. Third-party services

Google sign-in is provided by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Payment processing (if you purchase a plan) is handled by a payment provider (e.g. Razorpay) and is subject to that provider's privacy policy; we do not receive or store your card details.

5. Your control

You can revoke access at any time by signing out of the extension, removing the extension from Chrome, and/or revoking access in your Google account settings.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
