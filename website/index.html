Insta Unfollow

Followers & Unfollowers Tracker — a Chrome extension to see who does not follow you back, detect bot followers, and manage your Instagram connections.

Features

  • See who you follow but who does not follow you back
  • Detect suspicious / bot followers with a detailed score
  • Unfollow non-followers safely, directly from the extension
  • Whitelist accounts you want to keep

Privacy & Security

  • Your Instagram data is read only through your own logged-in session in your browser and is never stored on or sent to our servers.
  • Google sign-in is used only to identify you; your account information stays in your browser.
  • We do not sell or share any personal data.
