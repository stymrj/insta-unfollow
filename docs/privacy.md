# Privacy Policy

**Insta Unfollow** ("the extension") — made with love by Satyam Raj.

Last updated: August 18, 2026

## Overview

This extension helps you analyse your Instagram following list, detect accounts that do
not follow you back, find suspicious bot-like accounts, and unfollow accounts you no
longer want to follow. This policy explains what data the extension collects, why, and
how it is handled.

## Data we collect

### Google account (required to sign in)

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address,
profile picture and account identifier. This is used to identify you, to check your
membership status, and to protect your account.

### Instagram account data (required for the core features)

With your permission, the extension reads your Instagram profile, your following list
and your followers list, plus public profile information of accounts you scan (such as
username, profile picture, and follower counts). This data is used to:

- identify accounts that do not follow you back,
- detect suspicious bot-like accounts,
- manage your unfollow queue.

The extension can also perform unfollow actions on your behalf, strictly limited to the
accounts you explicitly choose.

### Usage data

The extension stores local counters (for example, how many accounts you scanned or
unfollowed today) to enforce free-plan limits and display statistics.

### Membership / payment status

If you purchase a premium membership, the payment provider we use receives the
transaction details. We store your membership status and expiry on our backend.

## Where data is stored

- **On your device:** most data (your scan results, counters, cached lists) is stored
  locally in your browser using Chrome's storage APIs. It never leaves your device
  except for the items below.
- **On our backend (Cloudflare Workers):** your email address, Instagram user id,
  membership status, and a record that your account exists. This is required so the
  admin console can manage memberships (grant, revoke, ban) and so your premium status
  can be verified across devices.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not share your data with third parties except the payment provider strictly
  necessary to process a purchase you make.
- We do not read your private messages, stories, or non-public content.
- We do not access your Instagram account without the actions you explicitly trigger.

## Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting
**sstymrj@gmail.com**. Your locally stored data can be removed at any time by
uninstalling the extension and clearing the extension's data in Chrome.

## Security

Premium membership is verified with cryptographic signatures issued by our backend;
locally stored membership cannot be forged. Communication with our backend is
encrypted (HTTPS).
